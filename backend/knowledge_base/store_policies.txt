# STORE POLICIES

## Shipping Policy

### Domestic Shipping (Within Country)
- Standard Shipping: 5-7 business days - $5.99
- Express Shipping: 2-3 business days - $12.99
- Free shipping on orders over $75

### International Shipping
- Available to select countries
- Delivery time: 10-15 business days
- Cost: Calculated at checkout based on destination

### Order Processing
- Orders are processed within 1-2 business days
- You will receive a tracking number once your order ships

## Return Policy

### Returns
- We accept returns within 30 days of purchase
- Items must be unworn, unwashed, and with original tags attached
- Return shipping is customer's responsibility unless item is defective

### Refunds
- Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the return
- Original shipping costs are non-refundable
- Refunds will be issued to the original payment method

### Exchanges
- We offer free exchanges for different sizes or colors
- Must be requested within 30 days of purchase
