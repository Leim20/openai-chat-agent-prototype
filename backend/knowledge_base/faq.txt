# FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

## Ordering

Q: How do I place an order?
A: Browse our products, select your size and color, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. Follow the prompts to enter shipping information and payment details.

Q: Can I modify or cancel my order?
A: Contact us immediately at contact@example.com. If the order hasn't been shipped yet, we can modify or cancel it.

Q: Do you offer gift wrapping?
A: Yes! Gift wrapping is available for $5 per item. Select this option at checkout.

## Shipping

Q: How long does shipping take?
A: Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days. Express shipping takes 2-3 business days. International orders take 10-15 business days.

Q: Do you ship internationally?
A: Yes, we ship to select countries. Shipping costs and delivery times vary by location.

Q: How can I track my order?
A: You'll receive a tracking number via email once your order ships. Use this number on our website or the carrier's website to track your package.

## Returns & Exchanges

Q: What is your return policy?
A: We accept returns within 30 days of purchase. Items must be unworn, unwashed, with original tags attached.

Q: How do I return an item?
A: Email contact@example.com with your order number and reason for return. We'll provide you with return instructions and a return label if applicable.

Q: Do you charge for returns?
A: Return shipping is the customer's responsibility unless the item is defective or we sent the wrong item.

## Sizing

Q: How do I find my size?
A: Check our size guide in the product description or on our Size Guide page. Measurements are provided for all our clothing items.

Q: What if the item doesn't fit?
A: We offer free exchanges for different sizes. Contact us within 30 days to arrange an exchange.

## Products

Q: Are your products true to size?
A: Yes, our products generally fit true to size. However, we recommend checking the specific product's size chart as fits may vary by style.

Q: How do I care for my clothing?
A: Care instructions are on the label of each garment. Most items can be machine washed in cold water and tumble dried on low.

Q: Do you restock sold-out items?
A: Popular items are usually restocked. Sign up for restock notifications on the product page or email us to inquire about specific items.

## Payment & Security

Q: Is my payment information secure?
A: Yes, we use industry-standard SSL encryption to protect your payment information. We never store your full credit card details.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.
